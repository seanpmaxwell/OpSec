# How to Erase Your AI Conversations and Search History

To make sure your AI conversations and related search histories are completely erased, clear them from **two distinct places** in your account. Deleting a conversation thread from an AI interface does not automatically wipe the underlying search log from your broader account history. [[1]](https://xtrusio.com/blog/google-ai-mode-history)

---

## Part 1: Clear the AI Conversation Threads

Choose the workflow that matches the AI tool you want to wipe.

### Google Gemini & Google AI Mode

1. Go to the Google Gemini home page. [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mqjI56ygd8)
2. **Delete individual threads:** Hover over a chat title in the left sidebar, click the three dots (**⋯**), and select **Delete**. [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mqjI56ygd8)
3. **Delete all AI history at once:**
   - Click **Settings & Help** (or the gear icon) in the bottom-left corner of the web interface.
   - Select **Activity** to open your AI activity control page.
   - Click the **Delete** button above your activity list.
   - Select **All time** (or **Always**) and confirm to permanently wipe every conversational prompt. [[1]](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/gemini-management-and-productivity/how-to-delete-gemini-chat-history-2026), [[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mqjI56ygd8), [[3]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXpPatXqj5s&t=20)

### ChatGPT & Other Alternatives

- **ChatGPT:** Click your profile icon → **Settings** → **Data controls** → **Delete all chats**.
- **Microsoft Copilot:** Go to the web dashboard, open **Settings** → **Personalization**, and clear your saved personalization memories and chat logs. [[1]](https://helpcenter.trendlife.com/en-us/article/tmka-13275/), [[2]](https://www.justwithai.com/safety/clear-chat-history)

---

## Part 2: Clear the Main Google Search History

To fully purge any trace of AI queries or standard searches from Google's servers, remove the logs from your master account dashboard. [[1]](https://www.security.org/digital-safety/search-history/google/), [[2]](https://xtrusio.com/blog/google-ai-mode-history)

1. Go to **Google My Activity**. [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxCD557QPlg)
2. Click **Delete** (right below the "Search your activity" bar). [[1]](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-delete-google-search-history)
3. Select your timeframe: choose **Delete all time** to wipe everything. [[1]](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-delete-google-search-history)
4. **Filter by product (optional):** A window will ask which products you want to clear.
   - To delete only AI and lookup records, check **Search** and **AI Mode** (if visible), and uncheck components like **YouTube** or **Maps**.
   - Click **Next**, review the sample history slated for removal, and click **Delete** to finalize. [[1]](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-delete-google-search-history), [[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FxCD557QPlg)

---

## Part 3: Prevent Future Tracking (Optional)

To avoid repeating these steps manually, you can configure your accounts to stop tracking entirely.

- **Turn off AI logging:** On your Google Gemini Activity Controls page, click the **Turn off** toggle next to **Gemini Apps Activity**. Select **Turn off and delete activity** to stop future tracking and clear past items at the same time. [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRhjm0IxY90&t=27), [[2]](https://helpcenter.trendlife.com/en-us/article/tmka-13275/)
- **Auto-delete search history:** On the main Google Activity Controls settings tab, find the **Auto-delete** toggle. You can set Google to automatically delete all search data older than 3 months. [[1]](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-delete-google-search-history), [[2]](https://www.security.org/digital-safety/search-history/google/)
